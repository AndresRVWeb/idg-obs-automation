## 1. Core Principles of YouTube Algorithm Optimization

YouTube's recommendation algorithm relies primarily on two main metrics:
1. **Click-Through Rate (CTR):** How many people click when the video is shown in Browse, Suggested, or Search. Driven by **Title + Thumbnail harmony**.
2. **Average View Duration (AVD) / Retention:** How long viewers stay tuned. Driven by content pacing, clear chapters/timestamps, and delivering on the title's promise.
